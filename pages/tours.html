Tour FAQ

Do I need a motorcycle license to drive on a tour?

For a Slingshot, no. They are registered as autocycles in Pennsylvania, so a regular driver license is all you need, and groups up to 6 guests ride Slingshots only. The 8-guest full-fleet tour also uses our Can-Am Spyder, and whoever drives that one does need a motorcycle endorsement. Mention it at booking and we will sort out who drives what.

How many people can come?

Two to eight guests. Each Slingshot seats two, so groups run one to four vehicles. Eight guests books the entire fleet.

How much is the deposit and when is the rest due?

A $250 deposit locks your date, and the remainder is due the day of the ride. Availability on the form is live, so a date that shows as open is genuinely open. Nothing is charged on the form itself.

What happens if it rains?

You reschedule free. We would rather move your date than run an open-air tour in the wet.

Is fuel extra?

The guide, the route, the coordination and the fuel for the guide vehicle are built into the tour price. None of it is a separate line item.

Can we split drivers during the tour?

Yes, as long as every driver is licensed and named before the tour starts. Most groups swap at the anchor stop so both people in a car get seat time.

Is this a good bachelorette or birthday party activity?

It is one of the most popular reasons people book. Each Slingshot seats two, so a group of six or eight takes most or all of the fleet and rides together the whole way. Most groups swap drivers at the anchor stop so everybody gets time behind the wheel.

Can we book a guided tour as a corporate team outing?

Yes. Two to eight people works well for a small team, and the day is genuinely shared: people pair up across a real drive, stop together for a long lunch, and come back having talked to each other. Tell us it is a work outing when you send the request and Chris will pace the day accordingly.

Can we rent multiple Slingshots as a group?

Group bookings run as guided tours rather than as several separate rentals. One price covers the whole group, Chris leads the route, and you get the vehicles you need: 1 vehicle for 2 guests, up to the full 4-vehicle fleet for 8. If you want a single vehicle on your own instead, that is a standard rental.

What is there to do in Lancaster, PA for a group of 6 or 8?

This is built for exactly that size. Eight guests books the entire fleet, which means the whole group is on the road together and nobody is left behind at a table. Both routes leave Lancaster County entirely, with a minimum of one hour of riding each way.
